press and hold to turn the dot into a sun
ice 100% · frozen
how it works
  • A small dot follows your cursor. While you hold the mouse button down (or keep a finger on the box), it is a sun; let go and it is a dot again.
  • Ice melts where the sun is close, so the side facing the sun goes first. Move around to free the soul.
  • Once it starts to melt, the ice keeps dripping and melting on its own, just more slowly.
  • Drops fall from the melting ice and the puddle grows.
  • When the ice is gone, the soul is free to wander around the box.
